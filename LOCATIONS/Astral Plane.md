# Astral Plane

Description: _An infinite plane that fits into no space. It is a realm of the mind, of thought, and of dream. There is no distance to travel or space to occupy, but the mind will not perceive it that way. Your physical form in the Astral Plane is just a manifestation of the mind._


- To move you need only thinking about where you want to go, and you will begin “flying” in that direction, though it is more customary and expedient to sail on vessels known as "Spelljamming" ships. 
- Time has no meaning; you won’t age, you won’t even experience hunger or thirst. 
- Home to settlements, like the [Rock of Bral](<./Rock of Bral.html>), built on fragments pulled from the material plane.
- There are dead or dying gods that have been slain or forgotten about floating within the Astral Sea., such as the dead god Havock, near the Rock of Bral settlement
- The Astral Sea also contains color pools that act as massive portals to the outer planes, and Wildspace realms where the Astral Plane borders and bleeds into entire solar systems in the Material Plane.
