# Eberron

Description:  _A world where they use magic to create wondrous technology. Warforged machines, ships that float in the air, and much more.

- Mythology: Eberron is actually the name for the land of the world and is referred to as the Dragon Between. Siberys, the Dragon Above, is the name given to the planetary rings which surround the planet. Khyber, the Dragon Below, is the name given to the underworld, similar to our Underdark.
	 - According to the creation story, the world was formed when three cosmic dragons battled and then changed their forms. One of the three, Khyber, sought dominion over reality, she fought Siberys, leading to Siberys' body being broken into pieces. To stop Khyber, Eberron coiled her body around Khyber, transforming into a living prison, the land of the world. Siberys' broken body became a ring around them both, and Khyber became the Underworld.

- Mournland: The Mournland is now a ruined wasteland. There was a global conflict waged across the major continent, Khorvaire, for over a century. It was known as the Last War. At the time the Mournland was the nation of Cyre, then an apocalyptic event called the Day of Mourning destroyed Cyre and transformed the region. There are reports of remnants of the Last War’s battlefields, such as the fire-spewing war machines known as colossi that lie strewn about the Mournland, constructs called warforged still wander the land, and it is supposedly crawling with monsters and bandits.

- Warforged: Created to fight in the Last War, they are constructs formed from wood and steel, then magically imbued with life and sentience.

- Magic: In the Mournland, magic doesn’t always function as intended, particularly teleportation and divination magic. It is supposed impossible to scry on anyone or anything within the boundaries. That may make it hard to impossible to track the item.

- Docents: Are small metal spheres, that were created to advise and assist warforged and other magical devices. You may be able to use one of these to boost the power of the first piece of the rod to home in on the next piece’s location.
