# Rock of Bral

Description: _A bustling port city built on an enormous asteroid. It’s filled with the most outlandish collection of merchants, mercenaries, pirates, nobles and entrepreneurs imaginable. It can be a bit lawless at times. For the most part the people adhere to two principles; mind your own business whenever possible, and enough gold can fix anything._

Key Locations:

1. Laughing Beholder
	- Most Notable Tavern.
	- Run and Owned by [Large Luigi](<../NPC's/Large Luigi.html>).

2. Mercane Agency
	- Run by the Mercanes (a merchant race)
	- Purchase of spelljamming helms and other magic items.
