
# Chapter 7 - Krynn

## MAJOR PLOT POINTS
1. **The [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) passed through into the world of Krynn and found a rising sun illuminating the rolling hills. In the fading dark of night they saw three moons; one  red moon, one white moon, and one barely visible black moon. The entered in to a grove of fruit trees bearing bright orange buds, and in the distance could make out a massive tree. It was towards this tree that the pieces of the Rod of Seven parts were pointing.**
	- Upon arriving at the enormously tall tree they found the branches bear and the bark beginning to rot, but the Rod pointed towards a hollow in the center of the trunk. As the LightningNuts entered the hollow they were assualted by giant spiders and an angry Treant, who provided little challenge. Near to death the Treant relented and pointed them towards a hidden grotto beneath the hollow. 
	- Entering the hollow the LightningNuts found a small Kinderfolk named Riffel who was searching for a fruit from the massive tree, called a Peylon Tree. He a member of a group sworn to protect Krynn from the forces of evil known as the Blue Fire Wardens. Recently they had led a disastrous assault on an evil stronghold, trying to reclaim a powerful artifact from the archmage Teremini, an ally of the death knight tyrant Lord Soth. Teremini, however, laid a trap and when the Blue Fire Wardens retreated they were hunted by a ravenous creature called a [borthak](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4468318-borthak). The Blue Fire Wardens were able to reach a nearby sanctuary, but the borthak is still attempting to break down the barrier to the sanctuary. Riffel needed a fruit as a means of distracting the creature to hopefully rescue his companions.
