
# Chapter 7 - Krynn

## MAJOR PLOT POINTS
1. **The [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) passed through into the world of Krynn and found a rising sun illuminating the rolling hills. In the fading dark of night they saw three moons; one  red moon, one white moon, and one barely visible black moon. The entered in to a grove of fruit trees bearing bright orange buds, and in the distance could make out a massive tree. It was towards this tree that the pieces of the Rod of Seven parts were pointing.**
	- Upon arriving at the enormously tall tree they found the branches bear and the bark beginning to rot, but the Rod pointed towards a hollow in the center of the trunk. As the LightningNuts entered the hollow they were assaulted by giant spiders and an angry Treant, who provided little challenge. Near to death the Treant relented and pointed them towards a hidden grotto beneath the hollow. 
	- Entering the hollow the LightningNuts found a small Kenderfolk named Riffel who was searching for a fruit from the massive tree, called a Peylon Tree. He a member of a group sworn to protect Krynn from the forces of evil known as the Blue Fire Wardens. Recently they had led a disastrous assault on an evil stronghold, trying to reclaim a powerful artifact from the archmage Teremini, an ally of the death knight tyrant Lord Soth. Teremini, however, laid a trap and when the Blue Fire Wardens retreated they were hunted by a ravenous creature called a [borthak](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4468318-borthak). The Blue Fire Wardens were able to reach a nearby sanctuary, but the borthak is still attempting to break down the barrier to the sanctuary. Riffel needs a fruit as a means of distracting the creature to hopefully rescue his companions.
	- As Riffel finished recounting his tale to the LightningNuts the [dryad](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4468321-deadbark-dryad) guardian of the Peylon tree, Gazaia, emerged from the shadows of the grotto. She demanded the party to leave the grotto, but agreed to give them a Peylon fruit in exchange for a magical item. Before leaving, the LightningNuts were able to convince Gazaia to tell of what happened to the tree. The dryad spoke of how Lord Soth's armies, led by Teremini, attacked the tree and stole a magic artifact embedded in the tree. Since then, the tree has been dying. To her shame, Gazaia hid in fear during the attack. Hearing this the LightningNuts felt the power of a new [secret](<../SECRETS LEARNED/SECRETS LEARNED.html>) being learned. 
2. **With the Peylon fruit in hand, the LightningNuts followed Riffel back towards the sanctuary of the Blue Fire Wardens. Arriving the group found the borthak viscously attacking the sanctuary door, and leapt into attack the creature, using the fruit to gain a momentary advantage over the creature. While the party fought the creature, Riffel slipped into the sanctuary to check on his people.**
	-  After vanquishing the borthak, the LightningNuts found the Blue Fire Wardens' sanctuary doors being opened, and Riffel walked out followed by small group of blue clad figures.
	- At the head of the group was a humanoid wolf-like creature, who introduced herself as Argentira Skywright. She welcomed the LightningNuts into the sanctuary and explained that she was a lycanthrope in hybrid form. Many of the other members of the Blue Fire Wardens, including Riffel, were lycanthropes while a few others were simply rangers. She then went on to describe the events their disastrous assault, saying,
		- _We learned of the artifact and the archmage’s plan to steal it but were too late to stop it. In our haste we assaulted their stronghold, the Three Moons Vault, in hopes of reclaiming it, but they knew we were coming. Their archmage leader, Teremini Nightsedge, created a barrier of “red moonlight” around the fortress and when we entered, we lycanthropes transformed and lost control, becoming nothing more than mindless animals. Many of our kind were killed in the assault and our leader, Valendar, was captured._
	- Sensing the stolen artifact was the next piece of the Rod of Seven Parts, the LightningNuts told Argentira that they planned to assault the Three Moons Vault stronghold, and hearing this she offered some aid in return for a favor, to rescue their leader Valendar. The LightningNuts, naturally agreed and Argentira gave them a [moonbeam](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619134-moonbeam) spell scroll, offered what knowledge they had of the fortress, and offered to send Riffel as guide to show the LightningNuts a less protected entrance to the fortress. 
	- The next morning, before the group departed for the Three Moons Vault, Argentira cast a protective spell on Riffel to make him temporarily immune to the forced transformation, and offered a few last warnings. 
		- First, she warned that Valendar may be under the effects of the red moonlight, and if so will mindlessly attack anything and anyone. If he is protected from the effects or weakened enough he should transform back to his human form. This would allow him to flee the fortress, and he could offer more knowledge of the what lays inside the fortress.
		- Second, she warned that the archamge Teremini should not be underestimated. She is a chief ally to the Death Knight Lord Soth, and was schooled in magic by an evil lunar dragon named Orinix. She will stop at nothing to destroy the Blue Fire Wardens.
		- Lastly, beware of drawing the focus of Lord Soth directly. He sticks to his keep in north, from were he directs his evil armies, but when he steps on the battle field he is an unconquerable force that wields the power of the cataclysm itself.  
3. **Warnings in mind, Riffel guided the LightningNuts to the Three Moons Vault, taking them along a small hidden path on the mountainside below the fortress. At the end of the path lay a entrance to the lower dungeon, guarded by two constructs with crescent moon-shaped heads. While the party snuck forward, Riffel, ever fearful, elected to remain outside on the path, waiting to escort Velander if he could be rescued.**
	- Dealing with the guards and dispelling a magical trap on the doors, the group entered the dungeon and made their way past a magically sealed barrier, using trial and error to determine the pattern of the runes needed to unlock the barrier. 
	- Past the door they found a heavily guarded dungeon. Wraiths wielding horns for alarm patrolled the halls, and as the invisible [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>) and [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>) scouted the rest of the dungeon, they discovered swarms of undead creatures lying in wait.
	- Khoaz, also discovered a locked cell door, behind which he could hear the distinctly howls of the lycanthrope leader of the Blue Fire Wardens, Velander. 
	- Trying a new tactic for the usually direct group of heroes, the LightningNuts attempted to sneak past the guards, hoping to free Velander, but [Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>) disguised as one of the [moonlight guardians](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4468333-moonlight-guardian) gave the group away when he attempted (as a creature without a mouth) to speak to one of the wraiths patrolling the halls. 
	- Realizing his mistake, Roman tried to grab the wraith but the guard dissolved to shadow and reforming immediately blasted his horn, raising the alarm. Deeper in the dungeon the LightingNuts heard the sounds of drums and the shrieks of the undead hordes rushing their way. 
	- [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>) however and slipped unawares up a tall spiral staircase, emerging into the courtyard of the fortress. Sneaking around the perimeter of the courtyard he scouted several key locations.
		- First he stumbled upon a large Orrery, showing the alignment of the three moons surrounding the world of Krynn. Studying the alignment he discerned that the **red moon (Lunitari) currently shines on the white moon (Solinari), that the white moon currently shines on the black moon (Nuitari), and that the black moon currently shines on the red moon.**
		- Climbing the stairwell of the Orrery, Wrick found a mirror emanating red moonlight, and sensing it might be important, took the mirror, covering it and slinging it across his back.
		- Leaving the Orrery, he explored other doors lining the courtyard, finding an armory, an empty barracks, and the bottom of the ruined white tower a large hole in the tower leading the outer defenses of the fortress. 
		- Sensing he had tested his luck enough, Wrick began returning stealthily back towards the dungeon staircase.
	- Meanwhile, in the dungeon some ingenious traps (both magical and mundane) laid by Khoaz and [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) were making quick work of the undead swarms of zombies and skeletons, while Roman and [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) dealt with the wraiths and massive ogre zombies. In no time the drums of the undead were silenced, and the only sound to be heard in the dungeon were the continued howls of the imprisoned Velander.
		- Opening the prison door, the LightningNuts were immediately rushed by an insane Velander in werewolf form, trapped beneath the light of a red moonlight mirror. Roman sprang forward hoping to grapple the creature, but the enraged werewolf proved too strong, breaking free. Luckily, Khoaz was quick of mind and bow, shooting the red mirror, destroying it.
		- As the red light faded, Velander calmed slowly transforming back to his hybrid form. After a moment of disorientation, he recovered enough to thank the LightningNuts and learning that they were their to aid the him and the Blue Fire Wardens, he offered as much knowledge about the Three Moons Vault as he could. 
			- He warned that Teremini was in the midst of a ritual, seeking to make the red moonlight barrier permanent. Hoping to use its effects to destroy the Blue Fire Wardens.
			- **She was using the artifact along with three infused lunar crystals to power the ritual. The crystals are tied to the magic of the three moons, and shine the light of an opposing moon on the each crystal should unbalance their power  and stop the ritual. Mirrors, similar to the shattered red one are located throughout the fortress and can be used to mimic the power of the three moon's light.**
			- He then gave the LightningNuts information about the fortress itself, detailing locations in the dungeon, each of the three towers, and Lunariums at the top of the tower. In particular he highlighted that Termini is performing her ritual in the Lunarium, that the Red Lunar tower holds an Orrery that shows the current positions of the moon, and that the Black Lunar tower is used by Teremini's necromancer Akaazi to create her undead armies. 
		- As Velander prepared to leave, Seraphine sensed a sadness emanating from the lycanthrope, and when prompted he confessed that, he underestimate Teremini, and her power. He felt the deaths of so many wardens were his fault, and the he should have scouted more, rather than leading his people hastily into a reckless assault. Hearing this the LightningNuts felt the power of a new [secret](<../SECRETS LEARNED/SECRETS LEARNED.html>) fill them. 
4. **Velander rescued, the LightningNuts began to explore the rest of the dungeon, before heading above to the fortress. It was around this time, that Wrick returned bearing the red moonlight mirror and information about what he had learned. (Naturally this coincided with the rest of the party finding some treasure).
	- The entire group made their way above into the main keep, and began exploring the rest of the towers. Entering the black tower, they found the necromancer, Akaazi, working on summoning more undead creatures for Lord Soth's armies. The LightningNuts sprung to the attack, and after defeating Akaazi they noticed a black moonlight mirror hanging overhead, which Seraphine flew up to procure. 
	- From there they snuck over to the remains of the ruined white tower, and made their way towards the top, where Roman kicked over some broken tiles to reveal a white moonlight mirror hidden below the floor of an old shrine. 
	- All mirrors in hand, the LightningNuts continued up towards the Lunariums and the Moondisk. As they exited the hatch to the white Lunarium, they saw the archmage Teremini in the midst of casting her ritual, as a dome of red moonlight slowly began to descend around the fortress. 
	- Taking a few precious seconds to organize their attack, the LightningNuts left Wrick in the white tower to use the red mirror, while Seraphine and Roman took the white mirror towards the black tower, and Hawk and Khoaz moved towards the red tower with the black mirror. The plan was solid, but things went wrong almost immediately. [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) having transformed into a ferocious (hat wearing) T-rex, immediately reverted to her true form when she stepped upon the Moondisk, while Khoaz found himself face to face with a fearsome undead beast called a [deathwolf](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4468322-deathwolf). 
	- Slowed by her forced reversion and mage duel with Teremini, Hawk, had to watch as Khoaz fell to the deathwolf, but as a dying act Khoaz was able to drop his mirror, shining the black moonlight on the red crystal. Wrick and Seraphine followed suit, dropping their mirrors to shine on their tower's crystals, and in a flash the ritual ceased. The magic powering the ritual, however, also powered the lunar Moondisk and as it disappeared, Hawk and Teremini began falling towards the courtyard below, but in a proper continuation of their mage duel they used magic to slow their descent, continuing their battle mid air. 
	- Meanwhile, Seraphine grabbed Roman and began flying him towards the falling deathwolf and getting close to the unconscious Khoaz was able to send him powerful healing. Throwing Roman towards the deathwolf, Seraphine continued to heal Khoaz, who near fully restored, then dove after the deathwolf, seeking revenge!
	- On the ground Hawk, and the now exhausted Wrick (having run down hundreds of flights of stairs), took on Teremini, and their combined magic was able to fell the archmage.
	- Just as her body burned in a flash of radiant power, the sky ripped open, and through the now open portal in the sky, a massive Lunar Dragon emerged. Orinix, the teacher of Teremini, has arrived.
	- To say the least, the lunar dragon battle was epic, but not surprisingly the LightningNuts were able to slay the adult dragon without suffering any major loss. 
	- However, as they retreated to the portal, ready to return to the Sanctum, there were cast into another vision. They saw Vecna continuing to break free of his radiant prison, the dark power of his Ritual of Remaking snaking out to consume other gods, including the bard goddess Philomena, and making its way towards the Tabaxi god, but the vision ended before the party could see his fate.
	- As they landed back in the safety of the Sanctum, they found it quiet. Even after taking their rest, they awoke to what seemed an empty space, but Roman awoke upstairs to find the Wizards Three, along with Eldon, casting the Wish spell to unmake the Sword of Kas. 
	- The others soon arrived, and they all witnessed the completion of the spell, the wizards all falling unconscious or exhausted and the sword clanging to the ground between them.
	- They learned from Alustriel, that they had removed the evil sentience powering the sword, weakening it slightly, but much of its power remained intact.
	- In addition, Alustriel presented Seraphine with a soul coin, Eldon finally able to unlock a way to see past Tiamat's barrier into Avernus. 
	- Seraphine was able to now access visions of the locations of the final two pieces of the Rod of Seven Parts. The first, was contained within a dark tomb on the Isle of Serpents on Hawk's home planet of Oerth. The other piece was in bright casino, called the Red Belvedere, sitting in the heart of Avernus.
	- After some deliberation, the LightningNuts decided to headed to Oerth and hunt for the entombed piece of the rod.
