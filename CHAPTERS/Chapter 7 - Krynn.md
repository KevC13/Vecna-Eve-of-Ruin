
# Chapter 7 - Krynn

## MAJOR PLOT POINTS
1. **The [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) passed through into the world of Krynn and found a rising sun illuminating the rolling hills. In the fading dark of night they saw three moons; one  red moon, one white moon, and one barely visible black moon. The entered in to a grove of fruit trees bearing bright orange buds, and in the distance could make out a massive tree. It was towards this tree that the pieces of the Rod of Seven parts were pointing.**
	- Upon arriving at the enormously tall tree they found the branches bear and the bark beginning to rot, but the Rod pointed towards a hollow in the center of the trunk. As the LightningNuts entered the hollow they were assaulted by giant spiders and an angry Treant, who provided little challenge. Near to death the Treant relented and pointed them towards a hidden grotto beneath the hollow. 
	- Entering the hollow the LightningNuts found a small Kinderfolk named Riffel who was searching for a fruit from the massive tree, called a Peylon Tree. He a member of a group sworn to protect Krynn from the forces of evil known as the Blue Fire Wardens. Recently they had led a disastrous assault on an evil stronghold, trying to reclaim a powerful artifact from the archmage Teremini, an ally of the death knight tyrant Lord Soth. Teremini, however, laid a trap and when the Blue Fire Wardens retreated they were hunted by a ravenous creature called a [borthak](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4468318-borthak). The Blue Fire Wardens were able to reach a nearby sanctuary, but the borthak is still attempting to break down the barrier to the sanctuary. Riffel needs a fruit as a means of distracting the creature to hopefully rescue his companions.
	- As Riffel finished recounting his tale to the LightningNuts the [dryad](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4468321-deadbark-dryad) guardian of the Peylon tree, Gazaia, emerged from the shadows of the grotto. She demanded the party to leave the grotto, but agreed to give them a Peylon fruit in exchange for a magical item. Before leaving, the LightningNuts were able to convince Gazaia to tell of what happened to the tree. The dryad spoke of how Lord Soth's armies, led by Teremini, attacked the tree and stole a magic artifact embedded in the tree. Since then, the tree has been dying. To her shame, Gazaia hid in fear during the attack. Hearing this the LightningNuts felt the power of a new [secret](<../SECRETS LEARNED/SECRETS LEARNED.html>) being learned. 
2. **With the Peylon fruit in hand, the LightningNuts followed Riffel back towards the sanctuary of the Blue Fire Wardens. Arriving the group found the borthak viscously attacking the sanctuary door, and leapt into attack the creature, using the fruit to gain a momentary advantage over the creature. While the party fought the creature, Riffel slipped into the sanctuary to check on his people.**
