# Chapter 10 - Kas and the War of Pandesmos

## MAJOR PLOT POINTS
1. **The party arrived back in the Sanctum exhausted, but took the time explain their most recent encounter to the Wizard's Three, while Seraphine attuned to the Rod of Seven Parts. Soon after they took a much needed long rest, but later into the night they fell into another disturbing vision of Vecna.**
	- In this vision, the radiant globe that surrounding Vecna is a spiderweb of thousands of black fractures and the space nearest is a roiling cloud of black energy. Barely half of the gods maintaining the barrier remain, the rest having been unmade by the power of Vecna’s Ritual of Remaking. The Lady of the Forest and Lastai still remain and for a moment Khoaz and Seraphine connect to them. Through that connection the two feel a desperate plea to hurry and get a sense that mere days, maybe even hours remain before the barrier finally breaks.
	- Hawk, however, is conspicuously absent from the vision. She finds herself instead being surrounded by a dark force and then feels a ripping in the fabric of reality as a dark portal begins to tear itself open in the room before her. Books, artifacts, plants, and furniture begin to swirl in a vortex of debris being sucked towards the portal and from the blackness steps the raven-haired form of the vampire Kas. He appears fully restored from the encounter in the jungles of Maztica, but for his left eye which now glows with an eerie pink light and his left hand which is shriveled and claw like.
		- In her horror, Hawk finds herself paralyzed and watches as Kas walks towards the unconscious Seraphine, stealing the Rod of Seven Parts. She then sees the Eldon and Wizard's Three confront Kas, but power emanates from his new Eye and Hand and Hawk witnesses Alustriel fall unconscious, Tasha scream in horror while necrotic energy overtakes her, Mordenkainen slowed to a near halt, and finally Eldon dissolve to dust.
		- Kas then steps through the portal thanking Hawk for the use of her connection and remarking he now goes to "finish what he started long ago".
		- As he departs Hawk senses the connection he spoke of and used the power of a secret to turn the connection against Kas. Through the reversal, she got a sense of his location and plan. She saw him step on to a plane of howling winds, dark streams and snow filled desolate caverns and knew he was now in Pandesmos, the first layer of Pandemonium, the realm of chaos. She snesed his plan to release the demon Miska the Spiderwolf, the ruthless and brilliant demonic general of the Queen of Chaos and was the creature first trapped by the Rod. She sees Kas and Miska lead a massive battle on the fields of Pandesmos, where the forces of Kas and Miska battle the forces of Vecna, aided by the Spider Queen Lloth. Finally she sees  Kas destroy Vecna and step into his place, completing the ritual and creating his own reign of terror across the multiverse.
	- As the party finally awakes, they relay what each of them saw and work to rouse the Wizard's Three but there is nothing left of Eldon to revive. Knowing time is short, they open a portal to Pandemonium and go on the hunt for Kas. 
2. **Stepping through the portal they find clifftop towering hundreds of feet over a beach, beyond which boils a sea of kaleidoscopic clouds. Corpses of many-legged horrors lie around the cliff top. From the corpse of a massive spider they watch four demonic fiends that look like spiders with wolf heads climb out and suddenly attack.** 
	- The party quickly deals with the demon wolf-spiders and while doing so were aided by a mysterious drow, who introduces herself as Vaeve.
	- In exchange for the promise of aiding her sister Vaeve, shows the party towards the cliffs edge and points out a massive war raging below. She points out that a huge tower on top of the cliff is where Vecna and Lloth's forces are being commanded and the points to a ruined citadel on the beach below. There, she states, is where Miska is currently be freed and Kas' forces are being controlled. Finally she points beyond both sites, and the battlefield, to a small structure. There is where Kas fled, to bunker within the cliffside. 
	- Vaeve asks them to help her sister who is trapped in the citadel, and suggests that taking out Miska and possibly the forces in the tower as well will create enough confusion to cross the battlefield unseen and therefore get to Kas. 
	- As the party debates about what to do next, the portal from the Sanctum glows and out walks the battered looking Wizard's Three leading a group of familiar faces. 
	- The large force contains Bruuz, Vlad, Cade and Mulwae, Kevori and some Neverwinter Guards, Kalyth leading a small band of veterans from Eberron, Concordia, Roman’s Sister, leading a few Tabaxi warriors, and band of Blue Fire Wardens, including Riffel who transforms into a massive werewolf nearly three times bigger than his normal size.
	- Assessing the situation Alustriel suggests that they handle the tower, while the party goes after Miska, Kas and the Rod of Seven Parts. From the tower they Wizard's Three would create a distraction amid the battlefield to buy the party time to get to Kas.
3. **Agreeing the LightningNuts made their way towards the ruined Citadel. They had little issues approaching, but had to sneak past a patrol to gain entry and soon found them fighting room after room filled with demon wolf-spiders of all varieties.**
