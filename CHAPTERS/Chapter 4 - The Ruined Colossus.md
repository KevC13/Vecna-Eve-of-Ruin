# Chapter 4 - The Ruined Colossus

## MAJOR PLOT POINTS
1. **Stepping through the portal from the Sanctum at [Sigil](<../LOCATIONS/Sigil.html>), the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) found themselves transported to the Mournland, a misty and magically ruined wasteland on the planet of [Eberron](<../LOCATIONS/Eberron.html>). Behind them stood the portal, a massive black mirror that reflected the misty valley below. Searching the landscape they saw the ruins of a massive mechanical creature and began making their way towards it.**
	- Arriving at the ruins of the mechanical creature, the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) spent a few searching the massive remains, the torso easily the size of a large house, and finally determined that the [docent](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/215652-docent) they were searching for had already been removed. 
	- [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>), in owl form, took to the sky and spotted another ruined machine nearby, but while en route the crew were attacked by evil warforged, loyal to the "Lord of the Blades". After the battle with the warforged, the LightningNuts took shelter in the torso of the colossal machine, and rested for the evening. 
	- The next morning, they discovered that the docent from this machine was also missing, and small sets of footprints were evident in the early morning light. 
	- Moving off in the direction of the footprints, the LightningNuts soon found a group of figures walking off in the distance. Approaching the group, they found a peaceful group of warforged pilgrims, led by a female named Mercy. After a brief friendly introduction, and a discussion about trying to find docents, the pilgrims invited the LightningNuts back to their village where they have a shrine to the small magical docents. 
	- At the village, Mercy asked for help dealing with a group of rival salvagers to the south, and in return the pilgrims would give them some valuable treasure they collected and allow them use of one of the docents.
