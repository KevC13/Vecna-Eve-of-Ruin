# Chapter 4 - The Ruined Colossus

## MAJOR PLOT POINTS
1. **Stepping through the portal from the Sanctum at [Sigil](<../LOCATIONS/Sigil.html>), the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) found themselves transported to the Mournland, a misty and magically ruined wasteland on the planet of [Eberron](<../LOCATIONS/Eberron.html>). Behind them stood the portal, a massive black mirror that reflected the misty valley below. Searching the landscape they saw the ruins of a massive mechanical creature and began making their way towards it.**
	- Arriving at the ruins of the mechanical creature, the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) spent a few searching the massive remains, the torso easily the size of a large house, and finally determined that the [docent](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/215652-docent) they were searching for had already been removed. 
	- [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>), in owl form, took to the sky and spotted another ruined machine nearby, but while en route the crew were attacked by evil warforged, loyal to the "Lord of the Blades". After the battle with the warforged, the LightningNuts took shelter in the torso of the colossal machine, and rested for the evening. 
	- The next morning, they discovered that the docent from this machine was also missing, and small sets of footprints were evident in the early morning light. 
	- Moving off in the direction of the footprints, the LightningNuts soon found a group of figures walking off in the distance. Approaching the group, they found a peaceful group of warforged pilgrims, led by a female named Mercy. After a brief friendly introduction, and a discussion about trying to find docents, the pilgrims invited the LightningNuts back to their village where they have a shrine to the small magical docents. 
	- At the village, Mercy asked for help dealing with a group of rival salvagers, a group of veterans, to the south, and in return the pilgrims would give them some valuable treasure they collected and allow them use of one of the docents. Agreeing, the LightningNuts made their way to the veteran camp the next morning. 
		- While [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>) was speaking with Mercy he was able to gain her trust enough so that she confided in him that she was separated from one of her friends, a blue and red warforged named Filch, on the Day of Mourning, Since then, her duty to lead the pilgrims has kept her from searching for her lost friend. Hearing this the LightningNuts felt the power of a new [secret](<../SECRETS LEARNED/SECRETS LEARNED.html>) learned. 
	- There they encountered the leader of the veterans, a female orc named Kaylth. At first Kaylth was hesitant to make a deal with with the pilgrims, needing to use the salvage to financially support the camp, but with some deft negotiating by [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>), the pilgrims and the veterans were able to come to an agreement. In the future they would offer mutual aid and protection, dividing the salvage among the two groups. 
2. **Having brokered a peace between the two groups, Mercy allowed the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) to use a working docent to tune one of the pieces of the [Rod of Seven Parts](<../SECRETS LEARNED/Rod of Seven Parts.html>) to the magic of the Mournland. Doing this allowed [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) to hone in on the location of the next piece of the Rod of Seven Parts.**
	- As the LightningNuts were leaving the village of Ialos, the veteran leader, Kalyth, pulled [Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>) aside. She assumed he was the leader (I mean why wouldn't she), and praised him for his ability to get the group to work together so well. She thanked him for bringing the veterans and pilgrims together, and offered aid in the future, should they ever need it. Lastly, she confided in him that she was personally relieved. She had lost numerous magics items along with some gold in the days following the destruction of the Mournland, and she had felt responsible for the veteran's current financial strain. Hearing this the party learned a new [secret](<../SECRETS LEARNED/SECRETS LEARNED.html>). As he was departing she used the phrase *Tomorrow in Cyre*, and told him it is a motto of hope for the future. 
3. **Following the tuned piece of the Rod of Seven Parts, the LightningNuts soon found themselves at the foot of a massive 300ft tall mechanical colossus, wedged into a cliffside. At the base of the cliff, they found a small cave and upon entering found dozens of bodies, long dead. They also found a pool of greyish liquid, and upon interacting with it saw twisted visions of themselves.
	- Naturally [Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>), following a phantom [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>), figured it would be best to split the party inside the dark, body-strewn, cave and soon came face to face with three monstrous and deformed creatures called [fomorians](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195000-fomorian).
	- Exploring deeper within the cave, [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>), [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>), and [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) found a path rising further up the cliff, and inspected a strange pool of the gray liquid that contained sparkling gems, but the misshapen weapons around the pool deterred them from direct contact, and  soon had turn back to aid the rage-crazed Roman.
	- With the aid of the rest of the party, the fomorians were soon vanquished, but not before Wrick suffered a psychic attack that left him somewhat exhausted.
	- Moving deeper into the caves, the group interacted with a couple of strange ghosts, one showing a vision of the Day of Mourning and how the colossal machine became lodged in the cliff.  
	- As they delved further, Khoaz discovered a sleeping [blazebear](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4468316-blazebear), and not to be out done by Roman, decided it would be a good idea to attack it rather that sneaking by. Inevitably, the LightningNuts were victorious, but only after taking a few hits themselves. 
4. **After killing the blazebear the LightningNuts continued exploring upward through the cave, being ambushed by a series of quick attacks by blade scouts as they moved closer to the top of the mechanical giant.**
	- Reaching the shoulders they found a map room and, upon inspecting the maps, the stone table beneath them came to life, requesting they speak a password. Just as it was about to attack, Khoaz remembered an old Cyran saying, _Tomorrow in Cyre_, that deactivated the stone golem, allowing them to take the valuable and historical maps. 
	- As they began to ascend higher, approaching the neck and head, the blade scouts attacked from above, using one of the defensive weapons of the large mechanical creature against them, shooting a blast of pressurized water at them as they climbed. Some of the LightningNuts dodged, others resisted the force of the blast, or in Seraphine's case she used the shower of water to reveal her "celestial self", spreading her wings and flying through the water to the top.
	- At the top the LightningNuts finally cornered the blade scouts and took care of the three nuisances. 
5. **From there, they were able to enter the head of the creature and found a massive ceramic brain being powered by a piece of the [Rod of Seven Parts](<../SECRETS LEARNED/Rod of Seven Parts.>). As they approach a swirling mass of grey liquid congealed in front of them, taking the form of a humanoid, and introduced itself as Landro the control system of the mechanical creature that bears the same name.**
	-  Landro was curious about the party and in exchange for answering their questions and transferring their consciousness to an "id crystal", they would help them remove the piece of the rod by shutting down the protective barrier around it. 
	- The LightningNuts back tracked to the map room and there found a chest (trapped of course) that contained an id crystal, a Manual of Bodily Health, a potion of Greater healing, and 2000GP.
	- Returning to Landro, they warned the LightningNuts that as soon as they transferred the barrier protecting the rod would shut down, and the mechanical creature would go into a self-destruct sequence.
	- Sure enough as they transferred, a pleasant voice sounded stating "1-minute until self-destruct". As the party fled the head, they decided to fly off the platform near the mouth of the creature, [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>) having to swoop down and catch [Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>) who had brazenly jumped off believing he could survive the 130 foot fall on strength and rage alone. 
	- The last minute grab allowed them to safely land, and the rest of the part soon followed.
