# CHAPTER 5 - DEATH HOUSE

## MAJOR PLOT POINTS
1. **Returning to Sanctum at [Sigil](<../LOCATIONS/Sigil.html>), the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>), weary from battle, rested. [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) experienced another flash of insight from the pieces of the [Rod of Seven Parts](<../SECRETS LEARNED/Rod of Seven Parts.html>) but this time only saw fire and ash and five pairs of eyes before awaking.** 
	- Unsure of what or where she was seeing, Seraphine shared her vision with the rest of the party, and [Alustriel Silverhand](<../NPC's/Alustriel Silverhand.html>) surmised that she may have glimpsed [Avernus](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Avernus), the top layer of the Nine-Hells. She also surmised that the eyes she saw may be those of the five-headed evil dragon queen [Tiamat](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Tiamat). 
	- Alustriel cautioned delaying pursuit of the rod piece on Avernus, until they could learn more. She also warned that Tiamat was not to be trifled with. As the queen, and progenitor, of the evil chromatic dragons, her power is even above that of [Vecna](<../NPC's/Vecna.html>).
2. **With Alustriel's warning, and better knowledge of the location, the LightningNuts decided to pursue the piece of the Rod Seven Parts, located in Barovia, the domain of vampire Count Strahd von Zarovich.**
	- [Mordenkainen](<../NPC's/Mordenkainen.html>), having been trapped previously in Barovia, informed them that the [Dark Powers](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Dark_Powers) have ultimate control over Barovia and the other Domains of Dread. Barovia in particular was created as an eternal prison for Strahd, and that the land is surrounded by an impenetrable mist. 
	- It will take him and Alustriel working together to open a portal to Barovia, but they will only be able to open in for 15 minutes at a time. They will open it each day at dawn, but can only do so for three days, after which they risk the Dark Powers notice and retribution. 
	- He also warned to avoid the notice of Strahd, if possible, he is extremely powerful, and even if defeated will only return within a day, fully restored. 
3. **Arriving in Barovia, the LightningNuts found a dreary land under a grey sky and entirely covered in fog and mist. As they entered the massive gates of Barovia and began walking along a dense forest lined road, they found themselves being watched by a large pack of dire wolves. Continuing forward the pack of wolves followed at a distance, until the LightningNuts made it to an open valley, where they could see a small village. Towering over the village, high upon a massive cliff, was a dark castle, the home to Strahd von Zarovich, Castle Ravenloft.**
	- Entering the village of Barovia, they soon found themselves surrounded by an angry mob, who were throwing rotten vegetables, calling them interlopers and thieves, and shouting for them to leave. Amazingly, the LightningNuts kept their composure, not threatened in the slightest by the mob, and they were able to calm them down and convince the villagers that they meant no harm. [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>), every eager for the spotlight, even played them a song, before they quietly dispersed.
	- As the LightningNuts continued through the village, being drawn to an ominous looking mansion, they were soon greeted by a figure from the shadows. The tall dark woman, carried a sword at her hip and announced herself as Sarusanda Allester, a member of the Ulmist Inquisition who are dedicated to rooting evil from throughout Barovia, and the wider multiverse.
	- Suggesting they move inside, away from prying eyes and ears, Sarusanda brought the party to the Blood on the Wine Tavern, a plain and nearly empty watering hole near the town square. There she explained that there are many evil forces at work within Barovia, not just Strahd and the Dark Powers, but lesser minions as well. 
	- One of these groups is a cult, led by Gustav and Elizabeth Durst, that venerates Strahd and performs rituals and blood sacrifices in his name. They own the ominous house in town, which is known colloquially as Death House. Recently they discovered a powerful magical artifact, but are unaware of what it is or how to use it. 
	- Unfortunately, a more malevolent group, necromancers known as the Priest of Osybus, have learned of this artifact, and she has been following them hoping for a chance to stop them or to destroy the artifact. 
	- Sarusanda explained that it was these priest who were responsible for turning the town against the LightningNuts. Somehow they knew of your imminent arrival, and tried to at least slow you down. The priests have infiltrated Death House, and Sarusanda suggested they could help each other in ridding the house of multiple evils. Any treasure, including the artifact, was the LightningNuts' to take.  
4. **Agreeing to help, the LightningNuts and Sarusanda, made their way towards Death House, and outside found two young children. The girl, named Rose, was trying to comfort her younger weeping brother, Thorn. Inquiring what was wrong, the children told the party that there is a monster in the house and their parents told them to wait outside. Before heading in, the party arranged for the children to be watched by a neighbor, and then entered.**
	- Exploring the first floor, they found little in the way of resistance, but did see wet cloaks in a closet, and found (and took) some finely made silverware. 
	- When [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) entered the den, she became incensed with rage at the sight of so many stuffed and mounted animals, that she immediately race upstairs, seeking vengeance. Kicking open a set of double doors, she found herself face to face with a few Priests of Osybus, and then all hell broke loose! Wrick hearing Hawk's battle cry rose the alarm of battle, and the rest of the party (except Wrick naturally) raced upstairs. As the fight ensued, the house came to life dropping a whirling and flaming chandelier on Hawk and awakening four suits of armor. 
	- The Priest of Osybus presented the toughest test. After a quick and deadly attack by Roman, the slain priest came back to life, while the others hit him with powerful magical weapons and spells, paralyzing him and preventing him from healing. While the rest of the party took care of the animated objects, Roman was left helpless. It was not until the rest of the party finally dealt with the Priest that Roman was freed from his inaction. 
	- Taking stock of the carnage of battle, Hawk noticed that her powerful lightning bolt had blasted open a portion of the wall, exposing a hidden staircase. 
5. **Post battle, the party split, exploring separate areas of the house. Seraphine and Khoaz, with Sarusanda in tow, entered the hidden staircase following it upwards, while Wrick continued their climb through the dumbwaiter. Hawk, began searching for secrets, while Roman, seeing another Priest of Osybus begin to rise, was raging in the library, pummeling the corpse into a pancake, then flipping tables and throwing chairs.**
	- As Khoaz and Seraphine approached the top of the hidden stairwell, they found a door and opening it saw another Priest of Osybus. The attacked from hiding and started another large fight as the room began to fill with other foes.
	- Meanwhile, Hawk noticed a hidden room behind a wall in the servants quarters and breaking through found a hidden chest (trapped of course) that contained blank spell books and a few spell scrolls. Stashing the loot, she soon made her way to the attic to aid Seraphine and Khoaz. 
	- Wrick climbing to the top of the dumbwaiter, exited into the master bedroom and (not surprisingly) found valuable jewelry that he pilfered. Soon after he discovered Roman (done with his raging temper tantrum) outside the room on the third floor. Roman sensing a battle was eager to join in, but could not find a means up until Wrick suggested the hidden staircase. Not long after Roman's departure, however, Wrick found another hidden staircase and joined the rest of the party in battling the Priests. 
	- After dealing with these foes as well, the LightningNuts explored the attic, finding a children's with a dollhouse replica of the Death House, and a creepy stuffed Strahd doll. From there they decided to descend the hidden staircase instead and soon found themselves in an underground crypt an ominous chanting echoing throughout. 
6. **Exploring the crypt, they quickly found a massive network of underground tunnels, with room carved out to serve as quarters and meeting areas for the Death House cult, complete with a shrine to the Lord Strahd von Zarovich (which Hawk burned to ash).**
	- After exploring the maze of tunnels, falling into some traps, and of course splitting the party, the LightningNuts stumbled upon the cultist in the act of performing a ritual sacrifice, a monstrous horned humanoid impaling a woman on a spear, tipped with the next piece of the Rod of Seven Parts. 
	- With the element of surprise and Wrick springing to action, most of the cultists were soon lost in the throes of the hit dance song, the Macarena, unable to react to the rest of the party attacking, including the cult leaders Gustav and Elizabeth Durst. 
	- The monstrosity, however, was unaffected and began teleporting throughout the room, impaling Wrick and sending deadly spikes shooting from his skin, the latter dropping Sarusanda.
	- Seraphine and Hawk soon arrived to reinforce the rest of the party, and while Seraphine went to battle, Hawk revived Sarusanda. Soon the Impaler was vanquished and it took little effort to the mop of the helpless, dancing, cultists.
	- While Hawk was talking to Sarusanda she sensed that the warrior was holding something back, and inquiring further discovered that Sarusanda's father is a Priest of Osybus who committed deplorable acts of violence. This inspired Sarusanda to join the Ulimist Inquisition in order to hunt him down and bring justice to those he hurt. Hearing this the LightnitingNuts felt the power of another secret learned.
7. **Room secured, Seraphine stepped upon the sacrificial dais and retrieved the fourth piece of the Rod of Seven Parts, and as soon as she did a cold wind blew through the crypt and the clear sound of the front door opening and locking greeted each of the party members.**
	- As the LightningNuts began leaving the house they found themselves facing a dark portal. Passing through they were teleported into a vision of Strahd at a dinner table. As they attacked Strahd he vanished to mist, and they only way they could escape the vision was to drink from a cup for a blood. Upon exiting, they found themselves dazed and transported to the dining room of Death House. 
	- As they opened the door to leave the dining room, they found themselves face to face with the real Count Strahd von Zarovich, flanked by two of his vampire spawn.
