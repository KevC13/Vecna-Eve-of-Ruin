# Chapter 6 - Maztica

## MAJOR PLOT POINTS
1. **Stepping through the portal the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) exited on the edge of a dense dark jungle. Before them was a wide open plain, where a small village bordering the jungle is visible in the distance. Taking in the scene, [Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>) immediately recognized the landscape as his home, the village of the Heated Plains Clan.**
	- Hesitant, but driven to discover what was going on in his homeland, Roman and the LightingNuts began to make their way towards the village, but soon heard, *"Hello little brother"* emanate from the nearby jungle followed by the emergence of Roman's sister [Concordia Purr](<../NPC's/Minor NPC's/Concordia Purr.html>). 
	- Concordia, relieved to see her brother, told of how their father, [Ares Purr](<../NPC's/Minor NPC's/Ares Purr.html>), went hunting, chasing rumors of the _Huetzi Ocēlōtl_ (the fallen god who takes the form of a dark Jaguar able to cloak themselves in darkness). On the hunt he stumbled upon some unknown and ancient ruins and there found a dark sword. At first it gave him strength. He seemed more powerful than ever, but the more he he wielded it, the more he darkened, becoming a twisted warlord. When Concordia confronted him, begging him to give up the sword, he refused and exiled her. A few of her loyal warriors followed, while the rest of the village were either enthralled by the darkness or cower in fear, unwilling to risk his wrath. Over the last few weeks, the leader of the garrison, father's brother [Racs Purr](<../NPC's/Minor NPC's/Racs Purr.html>), began recruiting outcasts and murders to form a "dark guard". These vile warriors seem drawn to the darkness of the sword and now rule over the village, while father spends most of his time at the ruins, sitting upon an old throne, dreaming madly of forming a new empire.
	- After hearing this, Roman was torn between rescuing the village and confronting his father. Concordia said that she and her warriors could try to barricade the garrison and free the villagers, if Roman could confront father and wrest the sword from him. Hopefully removing the sword would free most of the enthralled villagers.
	- Plan made, the two groups went their separate ways, and after a few hours of travel through the dense jungle, the LightningNuts found the ancient ruins, atop which sat Ares Purr and six of his dark guard. 
	- As Roman crested the top of the jungle ziggurat, Ares, wreathed in darkness, welcomed his son and then invited him, mockingly, to try and claim the throne and leadership of the clan. Then his dark guard attacked. The fight that followed saw swarms of ravens diving to attack the dark guard, [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) portaling between trees to attack from hiding, [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>), [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>), and [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>) attempting to distract the dark guard, while Roman flew forward to confront his father. 
	- After an intense battle, Roman and the LightningNuts were able to weaken Ares and then tear the sword from his grasp. As soon as he was separated from the evil weapon, Ares fell unconscious. 
	- For a moment all was calm and then out of the jungle the _Huetzi Ocēlōtl_ appeared, a massive (somewhat mishappen) jaguar wreathed in darkness. As it spewed darkness around the party, [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) summoned a massive tsunami to try and sweep the creature away, but just as the jaguar was engulfed by the wave it seemed to dissolve into mist.
	- The mist cloud seeped unaffected from the wave and when it reformed [Kas the Bloddy Handed](<../NPC's/Kas.html>), the once-general of Vecna, stood in its place.
