# Chapter 6 - Maztica

## MAJOR PLOT POINTS
1. **Stepping through the portal the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) exited on the edge of a dense dark jungle. Before them was a wide open plain, where a small village bordering the jungle is visible in the distance. Taking in the scene, [Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>) immediately recognized the landscape as his home, the village of the Heated Plains Clan.**
	- Hesitant, but driven to discover what was going on in his homeland, Roman and the LightingNuts began to make their way towards the village, but soon heard, *"Hello little brother"* emanate from the nearby jungle followed by the emergence of Roman's sister [Concordia Purr](<../NPC's/Minor NPC's/Concordia Purr.html>). 
	- Concordia, relieved to see her brother, told of how their father, [Ares Purr](<../NPC's/Minor NPC's/Ares Purr.html>), went hunting, chasing rumors of the _Huetzi Ocēlōtl_ (the fallen god who takes the form of a dark Jaguar able to cloak themselves in darkness). On the hunt he stumbled upon some unknown and ancient ruins and there found a dark sword. At first it gave him strength. He seemed more powerful than ever, but the more he he wielded it, the more he darkened, becoming a twisted warlord.
	- When Concordia confronted him, begging him to give up the sword, he refused and exiled her. A few of her loyal warriors followed, while the rest of the village were either enthralled by the darkness or cower in fear, unwilling to risk his wrath.
	- Over the last few weeks, the leader of the garrison, father's brother [Racs Purr](<../NPC's/Minor NPC's/Racs Purr.html>), began recruiting outcasts and murders to form a "dark guard". These vile warriors seem drawn to the darkness of the sword and now rule over the village, while father spends most of his time at the ruins, sitting upon an old throne, dreaming madly of forming a new empire.
