## MAJOR PLOT POINTS
1. **Upon returning to the Sanctum at [Sigil](<../LOCATIONS/Sigil.html>), [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) had a vision of the possible locations for more pieces of the [Rod of Seven Parts](<../SECRETS LEARNED/Rod of Seven Parts.html>)**.
	- The first location was a colossal skeleton floating in a silver-colored expanse with swirling mists and distant, star-like motes. Upon the remains of the skeleton was the wreck of a ship, and from the wreck [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) felt the pull of another piece of the Rod of Seven Parts.
	- The second location was in a ruined wasteland, fog shrouding the cliffs in the distance, and motionless metal colossal machines strewn about the shattered terrain. One of these colossi, dozens of times larger than the others, lay broken and resting upon a cliffside, and from it [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) felt the same pulse of a piece of the Rod.
2. **When she awoke [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>), relayed this information to the rest of the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>), along with [Alustriel](<../NPC's/Alustriel Silverhand.html>), [Eldon](<../NPC's/Minor NPC's/Eldon Keyward.html>), and [Mordenkainen](<../NPC's/Mordenkainen.html>).**
	- [Alustriel](<../NPC's/Alustriel Silverhand.html>) was able to identify that the first location was located in the [Astral Plane](<../LOCATIONS/Astral Plane.html>) in the body of a dead god, known as Havock.
	- After some research, [Eldon](<../NPC's/Minor NPC's/Eldon Keyward.html>) was able to determine that the second location was on the world of [Eberron](<../LOCATIONS/Eberron.html>) in a devastated region known as the Mournland.
3. **Having the clearest path to follow and location to explore, the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) chose to head to the Astral Plane first.**
	- [Alustriel](<../NPC's/Alustriel Silverhand.html>) was able to open a portal, close to the dead god Havock, to the floating trade city known as the [Rock of Bral](<../LOCATIONS/Rock of Bral.html>).
	- Arriving on the Rock of Bral the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) found a massive trade city built on a floating asteroid. While [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>), [Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>), and [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) went in search of items to buy, [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) and [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>) visited the Laughing Beholder Tavern, in hopes of chartering a ship to the dead god and the next fragment of the [Rod of Seven Parts](<../SECRETS LEARNED/Rod of Seven Parts.html>).
	- Upon entering the tavern, [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) and [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>) found a lively atmosphere and were soon greeted by the tavern's owner and namesake, a boisterous and good natured beholder, Large Luigi.
	- Large Luigi was happy to help the two in their search for a ship, and pointed out two seasoned captains currently within the tavern. 
		- One was a half-elf named Captain Ned Ian Fairmorn, who was said to be honest and never fails to finish a job on time. He sails a small fast ship, the _Wandering Dream_, that is lightly manned and lightly armored. 
		- The other captain was a githyanki named Captain D'raaki. He has a fearsome reputation and typically skirted the line between pirate and mercenary. He owned the _Grimmdrak_, a large well manned galleon. Large Luigi warned that, while there would be no threat of pirate attacks aboard the _Grimmdrak_, Captain D'raaki and his crew just as likely to turn on you as they were to finish the job. 
	- After interviewing both captains, the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) decided to hire Captain Fairmorn, but [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>) got the feeling that Captain D'raaki and his crew might try to follow and waylay them. Wanting to avoid a pirate attack, [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>) paid to get the crew drunk, and Captain Fairmorn arranged to have the _Grimmdrak_ searched for illicit cargo by paying off a dock worker. 
4. **Ship chartered the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) sailed into the Astral Sea, encountering a couple of massive [Star Anglers](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4468339-star-angler) enroute to the dead god Havock. Finally arriving at their destination, they found the remains of a wrecked spelljamming ship strewn about the skeleton of the dead God, and led by the pull of the [Rod of Seven Parts](<../SECRETS LEARNED/Rod of Seven Parts.html>) flew towards the prow of the ship, now impaled in the heart of the dead god.** 
	- After landing on the prow, and [Hawk's](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) excessive use of force to open a locked door, the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) found the captain of the wrecked ship, Captain Inda, in her now shattered quarters.
