# Chapter 3 - The Lambent Zenith

## MAGIC ITEMS/TREASURE FOUND

2. [Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>) purchased a red [Cloak of Billowing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/27040-cloak-of-billowing)

## MAJOR PLOT POINTS
1. **Upon returning to the Sanctum at [Sigil](<../LOCATIONS/Sigil.html>), [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) had a vision of the possible locations for more pieces of the [Rod of Seven Parts](<../SECRETS LEARNED/Rod of Seven Parts.html>)**.
	- The first location was a colossal skeleton floating in a silver-colored expanse with swirling mists and distant, star-like motes. Upon the remains of the skeleton was the wreck of a ship, and from the wreck [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) felt the pull of another piece of the Rod of Seven Parts.
	- The second location was in a ruined wasteland, fog shrouding the cliffs in the distance, and motionless metal colossal machines strewn about the shattered terrain. One of these colossi, dozens of times larger than the others, lay broken and resting upon a cliffside, and from it [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) felt the same pulse of a piece of the Rod.
2. **When she awoke [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>), relayed this information to the rest of the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>), along with [Alustriel](<../NPC's/Alustriel Silverhand.html>), [Eldon](<../NPC's/Minor NPC's/Eldon Keyward.html>), and [Mordenkainen](<../NPC's/Mordenkainen.html>).**
	- [Alustriel](<../NPC's/Alustriel Silverhand.html>) was able to identify that the first location was located in the [Astral Plane](<../LOCATIONS/Astral Plane.html>) in the body of a dead god, known as Havock.
	- After some research, [Eldon](<../NPC's/Minor NPC's/Eldon Keyward.html>) was able to determine that the second location was on the world of [Eberron](<../LOCATIONS/Eberron.html>) in a devastated region known as the Mournland.
3. **Having the clearest path to follow and location to explore, the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) chose to head to the Astral Plane first.**
	- [Alustriel](<../NPC's/Alustriel Silverhand.html>) was able to open a portal, close to the dead god Havock, to the floating trade city known as the [Rock of Bral](<../LOCATIONS/Rock of Bral.html>).
	- Arriving on the Rock of Bral the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) found a massive trade city built on a floating asteroid. While [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>), [Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>), and [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) went in search of items to buy, [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) and [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>) visited the Laughing Beholder Tavern, in hopes of chartering a ship to the dead god and the next fragment of the [Rod of Seven Parts](<../SECRETS LEARNED/Rod of Seven Parts.html>).
	- Upon entering the tavern, [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) and [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>) found a lively atmosphere and were soon greeted by the tavern's owner and namesake, a boisterous and good natured beholder, Large Luigi.
	- Large Luigi was happy to help the two in their search for a ship, and pointed out two seasoned captains currently within the tavern. 
		- One was a half-elf named Captain Ned Ian Fairmorn, who was said to be honest and never fails to finish a job on time. He sails a small fast ship, the _Wandering Dream_, that is lightly manned and lightly armored. 
		- The other captain was a githyanki named Captain D'raaki. He has a fearsome reputation and typically skirted the line between pirate and mercenary. He owned the _Grimmdrak_, a large well manned galleon. Large Luigi warned that, while there would be no threat of pirate attacks aboard the _Grimmdrak_, Captain D'raaki and his crew just as likely to turn on you as they were to finish the job. 
	- After interviewing both captains, the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) decided to hire Captain Fairmorn, but [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>) got the feeling that Captain D'raaki and his crew might try to follow and waylay them. Wanting to avoid a pirate attack, [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>) paid to get the crew drunk, and Captain Fairmorn arranged to have the _Grimmdrak_ searched for illicit cargo by paying off a dock worker. 
4. **Ship chartered the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) sailed into the Astral Sea, encountering a couple of massive [Star Anglers](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4468339-star-angler) enroute to the dead god Havock. Finally arriving at their destination, they found the remains of a wrecked spelljamming ship strewn about the skeleton of the dead God, and led by the pull of the [Rod of Seven Parts](<../SECRETS LEARNED/Rod of Seven Parts.html>) flew towards the prow of the ship, now impaled in the heart of the dead god.** 
	- After landing on the prow, and [Hawk's](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) excessive use of force to open a locked door, the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) found the captain of the wrecked ship, A Deva named Captain Inda, in her now shattered quarters. 
	- After they aided Captain Inda in repairing her prosthetic wing, she revealed that the next piece of the [Rod of Seven Parts](<../SECRETS LEARNED/Rod of Seven Parts.html>) was sealed behind a protective magical barrier, and that the only way to deactivate the barrier was to disable two runes, located on the stern and starboard segments of the broken ship. 
		- The runes are invisible to the naked eye and in order to deactivate the runes they would need to speak the phrase, "The moon sings a song for the lost".
		- The rune on the stern is located on the headboard of the bed in the ship's stateroom. 
		- The other is located on the wall above her desk in the study on the starboard segment of the ship. 
	- Captain Inda's final request was to aid her in finding and uniting her crew, if any of them survived the wreck. 
5. **As the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) began to travel across the gap to the starboard segment, they were suddenly attacked by to monstrous shark-like creatures called Night Scavvers. While the battle raged, [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) made her way to the starboard segment and found a large [treant](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/mm-2024/monsters-t#Treant), named Redbud, anchored to the deck of the wrecked portion of the ship.**
	- Hawk learned from Redbud that a large number of parasitic creatures were below decks, blocking the way to the location of the first rune and trapping his friend, the [blink dog](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4830980-blink-dog) Ikasa. Undeterred, Hawk jumped down in to the ship ready to confront the monsters.
	- After dealing with the night scavvers, the rest of the LightningNuts followed Hawk into the starboard segment of the ship, and soon dealt with parasitic monsters. Sometime during the fracas, however, two [Wricks](<../PC's/Wrick.html>) appeared, the new Wrick (aka [Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>)) still quite deft at dancing, but having no skill with music.
	- [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) used the time during and after the battle to talk with Ikasa, and learned that they had crashed on a life boat after being attacked by pirates. One of their friends, Daveras, was trapped on another part of the ship. Pressing further, Ikasa was also saddened because she knew that another member of their crew fell overboard during the attack and is lost, floating/flying somewhere out in the Astral Sea. Ikasa is the only one who knew this, and she has been wracked with guilt while trapped in the ship, unable to tell anyone about it. 
		- Hearing this the party felt the power of a new [secret](<../SECRETS LEARNED/SECRETS LEARNED.html>) being learned.
	- Exploring the study, [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>), found a lockbox hidden within a book and while fiddling with it unleashed a small burst of poison gas in his face, the consolation for this endeavor being a [potion of mind reading](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228936-potion-of-mind-reading) and Captain Inda's journal. 
	- Meanwhile, [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) said the deactivation phrase "The moon sings a song for the lost" and the first hidden rune burned out above the desktop in the study.
6. **Having deactivated the first rune, the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) made their way to the stern of the ship, and where greeted by a couple of friendly but strange githyanki crew members, one of which who went off to fetch the interim leader, the hippo-esque giff named Ilren. 
	- During a brief conversation with Ilren, the LightningNuts learned that the first mate, Figaro, had locked himself in his quarters suffering from an "illness". 
	- Sensing something off about what Ilren was telling them, [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>), [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>), and [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>) went to explore below decks for the ailing first mate, while [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) and [Wrick-Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>) stayed with Ilren to question him further. 
	- Below decks, [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>), [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>), and [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>) found some crew resting, the ship's cook (who had a hankering for a Khoaz meal), a the blink dog's friend Daveras. Daveras described the first mate as paranoid, rather than ill, and agreed to show the group where he was located, in hopes that they could help him. 
	- [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>), [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>), and [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>), with Daveras' help, were able to gain entry into Figaro's stateroom and found the first mate strangely out of it. Understanding that Figaro was teh victim of the [modify memory](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2196-modify-memory) spell, Seraphine cast [remove curse](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618943-remove-curse) and was able to clear the fog from his mind. As Figaro snapped out of it, he quickly exclaimed that Ilren was an imposter who had cursed him, and then he ran out exclaiming he was going to "kill him!". 
	- The more [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) and [Wrick-Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>) spoke with Ilren, the more they began to suspect he was hiding something. As they were following him "checking on the crew", Figaro sprang forward and suddenly blasted Ilren a spell attack. As the attack landed, Ilren transformed into a hideous [death slaad](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5194964-death-slaad) and the crew joined the LightningNuts of ridding the ship of this evil monster. 
7. **After the battle, [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) deactivated the second rune in the stateroom, while [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>) uncovered a hidden trap door leading to a brig. In the brig was another crewmember, Cirit who was locked away because she was aware of Ilren's evil nature. With both runes now deactivated, the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) returned to the prow of the ship, reuniting the crew with Captain Inda, and descended into the now accessible heart of Havock.**
	- Moving into the heart, the LightningNuts discovered a monstrous snake-like creature, known as a [hertilod](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/4468328-hertilod) asleep and clinging to the ceiling of the dying heart!
	- During the ensuing battle, the monster shook off magical attacks with ease, threw poisoned spikes, and even tried to swallow [Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>) and [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>), but the LightningNuts would not be shaken, and ultimately [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) was able to land the killing blow causing the hertilod to regurgitate up the second piece of the [Rod of Seven Parts](<../SECRETS LEARNED/Rod of Seven Parts.html>).
8. **Returning victorious to the deck of the ship, the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) were rewarded by Captain Inda for their efforts, and her first mate Figaro joined The LightningNuts for their return voyage, in order to organize a rescue transport.**
	- On the return journey, [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>) and [Wrick](<../PC's/Wrick.html>) noticed that the first mate, Figaro, was unsettled and learned that he knew about the dangers of the area of the Astral Sea where his spelljamming ship crashed but deliberately hid this information from the captain and crew hoping that the possible rewards would be worth the risk. Hearing this they knew that is was another [secret of power](<../SECRETS LEARNED/SECRETS LEARNED.html>). 
	- One evening, while [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html) was sleeping, she dreamt fitfully. Her dreams were filled with flashes of Kas searching for his lost sword. After seeing him in numerous locations, Hawk found him is walking along a crater-marked landscape, with a glaring red sun hanging in a black sky, when he suddenly stopped, turned abruptly and looked directly at her, smiling. Hawk suddenly awoke in a sweat, left wondering what the dream meant.
	- In the meantime, [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) spent the return trip "growing closer" to Captain Ned Fairmorn. (and yes growing closer mean sex)
9. **Arriving back at the [Rock of Bral](<../LOCATIONS/Rock of Bral.html>), the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) bought and sold some treasure, caught up with [Large Luigi](<../NPC's/Large Luigi.html>), and even discovered a monster hunting organization called Xenotermination Limited.**
	- [Roman](<../PC's/Roman.html>), [Khoaz](<../PC's/Khoaz.html>), and [Hawk](<../PC's/Hawk.html>) were tempted to stay and take Urksedda Ulfand, the grizzled dwarf leader of Xenotermination, up on her offer of a "trial" hunt, but the weeks long wait and the need to continue hunting [Vecna](<../NPC's/Vecna.html>) took priority. 
10. **Taking the portal back to the Sanctum at [Sigil](<../LOCATIONS/Sigil.html>), the LightningNuts returned to find that very little time had passed during their expedition to the Astral Plane. [Alustriel](<../NPC's/Alustriel Silverhand.html>), asked that they give her until morning to research and ready a portal to their next location, and [Eldon](<../NPC's/Minor NPC's/Eldon Keyward.html>)  was excited to learn of their latest adventures.**
	- During their first long rest back in the Sanctum, [Seraphine](<../PC's/Seraphine.html>) once again connected with the fragments of the [Rod of Seven Parts](<../SECRETS LEARNED/Rod of Seven Parts.html>), this time seeing a gloomy village, shrouded in mist. In the village she saw one house in particular that radiated a sense of evil, feeling the pull of the next piece of the Rod of Seven parts coming from within. As the vision fade the name of the town sprang to mind, the Village of Barovia.
	- The next morning, [Mordenkainen](<../NPC's/Mordenkainen.html>) warned that Barovia is a land both ruled by and prison to an ancient vampire of deadly power, [Count Strahd von Zarovich](<../NPC's/Strahd.htmk>). He cautioned patience, as traveling to Barovia will be both challenging and possibly deadly to those not yet strong enough and prepared to face such a deadly foe. 
	- With this warning, the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) decided to stick with their original plan and seek the piece of the Rod located with in the realm of Ebberron.
