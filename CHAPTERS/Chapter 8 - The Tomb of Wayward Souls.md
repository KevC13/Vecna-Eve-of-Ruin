# Chapter 8 - The Tomb of Wayward Souls

## MAJOR PLOT POINTS
1. **The closest portal to the tomb, was found in the Free City of Greyhawk, one of the greatest cities on Oerth, and Hawk's childhood home. To save time, Alustriel directed the [LightningNuts](<../PC's/LightningNuts.html>) to a colleague who was in charge of the Great Library, a dragonborn mage and head librarian, Abra.**
	- After resupplying the LightningNuts went to the library and were able to convince Abra to teleport them to the Isle of Serpents.
	- Upon arrival at the island, they found an expedition of archeologists under attack by a massive [Kraken](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/5195097-kraken). Naturally the LightningNuts jumped to aid them, Roman nearly getting eaten during the battle.  
	- After the battle, a couple of the expedition leaders rushed to the party in thanks; Laysa, the lead archeologist and a descendent of the island, and Marian, the expedition's arcana expert and Abra's mentee. The two explained that they were here studying the tomb at the heart of the island, and asked if the group would be willing to enter the tomb and make it safe for study. They offered important details regarding it's nature and location.
		- The tomb is known as the Tomb of Wayward Souls. 
		- It was built by the evil archlich [Acererak](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Acererak), after he attacked the inhabitants of the island and stole all their treasures. He is infamous for building elaborate death traps in these tombs, and then hiding valuable treasures within the deadly corridors. 
		- Laysa, believes many of her ancestral treasures are within the walls of the tomb, and is hoping to reclaim them. She offers 500GP per item that can be reclaimed. She describes them as;
			- An ebony wand decorated with bones and feathers.
			- A ring of golden stars.
			- A sacred scimitar dedicated to serpent spirits.
			- A set of copper tablets engraved with incantations.
			- A crystal orb used in rituals.
			- A blue silk sash used in ceremonies.
		- Marian, is fascinated by the lore of the tombs and Acererak, and is willing to give her [Lantern of Revealing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228811-lantern-of-revealing) and [Gem of Seeing](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9228653-gem-of-seeing), in exchange for any information regarding the tombs layout, traps, history, and on Acererak himself. 
		- Lastly, the two shared that they had explored and excavated the entrance to the tomb already, but have not determined how to safely enter the tomb. The provided a [map of the tomb entrance](<../IMAGES/map-7.02-tomb-complex.png>), with a description of what dangers they had already uncovered. 
	- With this information in hand, the party agreed to head into the tomb after a good night's rest. 
	- What they found, was unsurprising. Traps, puzzles, cursed jewelry, statues springing to life to attack them, floating [spheres of annihilation](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9229086-sphere-of-annihilation) following them menacingly, these and so much more littered the halls of the Tomb of Wayward Souls. Among some of the key discoveries were;
		- Two of the treasures Laysa had mentioned; the sacred scimitar (a [scimitar of sharpness](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9229114-sword-of-sharpness) and the engraved copper tablets (a [manual of bodily health](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/4674-manual-of-bodily-health)).
		- A spell scroll of [Greater Restoration](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618961-greater-restoration). 
		- A leather bound journal that described the combination to the crypt doors as the word "DIE" in draconic.
	- Moving further into the tomb, the found a hidden lab were Acererak was attempting to make simulacra of himself. In a hidden drawer within his desk, they found a journal chronicling his efforts. He detailed his repeated failures, but also spoke of his goal with disdain and equated a simulcra of himself as nothing more than sadistic joke. 
	- Beyond the hidden lab they found a room filled with an underwater illusion, and after piercing through the veil of the illusion were able to gain access to the entrance to the crypt chamber. Here they found a massive door with dials that had the draconic alphabet etched into them, and when they set the dials to spell the word DIE, the crypt doors opened. 
	- Within the tomb they found Acererak's simulacrum, Rerak. While the false lich did not want to follow his creators commands, the power over him compelled him to attack the party, send Wrick and Roman in to an unconscious state, while he raged a fierce fight with the others.
