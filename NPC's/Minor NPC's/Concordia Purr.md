# Concordia Purr

Role: Sister of [Roman](<../../PC's/Roman.html>)

Notes:
- A powerful warrior of the Heated Plains Clan.
- She stood up to her father, [Ares Purr](<./Ares Purr.html>), when he became possessed by the [Sword of Kas](<https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/9229112-sword-of-kas>) and was exiled for it.
- She now leads a small group of loyal warriors who are hoping to free her father and the village from this evil.
