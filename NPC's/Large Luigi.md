# Large Luigi

Description: A large lawful neutral boisterous beholder who runs the Laughing Beholder in the [Rock of Bral](<../LOCATIONS/Rock of Bral.html>). He is happy to chat with patrons and is very knowledgeable about the Rock of Bral and the people who dwell there.
